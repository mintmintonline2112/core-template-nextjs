Almonds for Wholesale, Distribution & Food Manufacturing

Prime Nuts USA offers California almonds for commercial and industrial applications — natural almond kernels in the varieties, sizes, and grades your market requires.

Varieties

  • Nonpareil

    The flagship California variety — light color and a smooth, attractive kernel, favored for premium snacking and retail programs.

  • Carmel

    A versatile kernel well suited to roasting, blanching, and food-manufacturing applications.

  • Monterey
